# Living Hall + Pooja Corner — Final Review Set V2

This set is a design-review package based on the architect plan and the approved L1/L2/L3 + P1/P3 combination. The images are concept renders, not construction-issued drawings.

## Controlling dimensions and decisions

- Hall: 12 ft 6 in × 18 ft 1.5 in.
- TV: 75 in; target screen centre 1100–1150 mm above finished floor.
- Sofa: L-shaped, modular internally, visually continuous seat plane with no visible seat divisions.
- Sofa service board: top about 650 mm above floor and below the approximately 760 mm sofa back; charging modules at exposed ends only.
- Pooja: 42 in wide × 13.5 in deep × approximately 84 in high.
- Pooja inventory: 8 photos on two rails and 7 idols on a removable two-tier stone riser.
- Pooja doors: four narrow fluted/frosted-glass leaves; each pair folds, pivots and retracts into a side pocket.
- Diya: one live flame only, on the fully extended stone-lined tray; never close the doors until flame/incense is extinguished and cooled.

## Render index / final prompt set

- F01: measured overhead; show the actual plan, furniture footprints and circulation.
- F02: main entry view; L1 room character, L2 TV/charging joinery and L3 artwork.
- F03: sofa elevation; continuous sofa, low service board, end charging points and botanical art.
- F04: TV elevation; correctly scaled 75-inch TV, restrained stone/fluting and ventilated hidden console.
- F05: seated viewing perspective; verify TV height and viewing distance.
- F06: reverse corner; verify chaise termination and clear bedroom/kitchen route.
- F07: living/stair/pooja relationship; keep the compact pooja separate from the TV feature wall.
- F08: pooja open; four leaves parked, 8 photos, 7 idols and one flame on the extended tray.
- F09: pooja closed; tray retracted, diffused electric glow only, no flame or smoke.
- F10: flame-free three-stage folding-pocket door operation and hardware concept.
- F11: diya tray/service detail; 25 kg runners, positive stop, non-combustible top, ventilation and accessible service chase.
- F12: dimensioned pooja joinery concept sheet with open/closed elevations, sections and safety note.

## Before fabrication

The carpenter and hardware supplier must produce coordinated shop drawings and a full-size door-mechanism mock-up. Confirm actual photo-frame widths, idol bases, wall conditions, electrical loads, hardware clearances and stone thickness on site. The folding-pocket hardware shown is a design intent; select a manufacturer-rated system and obtain installer sign-off. A licensed electrician must verify the concealed power, ventilation and isolation arrangement. No live flame is permitted behind closed doors.
