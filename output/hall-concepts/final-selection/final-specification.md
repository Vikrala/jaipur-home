# Selected Hall and Puja Design

The architect plan remains the binding dimensional reference. The images are the selected visual direction; final fabrication must follow measured shop drawings.

## LF1 FINAL — Living room

### Selected combination

- Overall layout, palette, sofa, rug, table and simple ceiling: L1.
- TV wall and low wooden service plank behind the sofa: L2.
- Pair of botanical wall-art frames: L3.

### Fixed dimensions and requirements

- Hall: 12 ft 6 in × 18 ft 1.5 in.
- Continuous L-shaped sofa: approximately 11 ft main run × 4 ft 6 in return, 34–36 in deep.
- Seat upholstery: one visually uninterrupted bench plane with no seat divisions. Any transport/fabrication split must be concealed below the visible upholstery plane.
- Sofa back: approximately 30 in high.
- Walnut service plank: top at 25–26 in above finished floor, always below the sofa back.
- Electrical modules: one accessible black module at each end, each containing an Indian 6A socket, USB-A and USB-C charging.
- Lighting: dimmable 2700K LED in a concealed rear channel to wash the wall upward; driver placed in an accessible ventilated box.
- TV: 75 in diagonal, approximately 65.4 in wide × 36.8 in high.
- TV console: approximately 8 ft 6 in wide × 14–15 in deep.
- TV services: ventilated removable equipment bay, cable raceway, spare conduit, service loop and IR repeater. No visible switchboard or loose wiring.
- Coffee table: approximately 42 × 20 in.
- Target clear movement around the table: minimum 36 in; the selected envelope permits approximately 39.5 in on each long side.
- No dining table, sink, handwash or additional accent chair in the hall.

## PF1 FINAL — Puja corner

### Selected combination

- Walnut, ivory stone, brass detailing, photo rails, idol riser, lighting and ventilation: P1.
- Flush retracting door pockets and fully extendable diya tray: P3.

### Fixed dimensions and inventory

- Overall envelope: 42 in wide × maximum 13.5 in deep × approximately 84 in high.
- Doors: four approximately 9 in-wide P1-style fluted-glass leaves in brass/walnut frames. The two leaves on each side fold together, pivot 90 degrees, then retract backwards into a flush side pocket. The reverse sequence pulls each pair forward before unfolding and closing it. No leaf remains in the adjacent circulation or 32 in door path.
- Door zone: the doors close only across the shrine opening. The independent diya tray and both drawers remain below the door line and externally accessible.
- Photos: exactly eight frames on two shallow rails, four per rail. The tallest frame is 9 in; smaller frames include 3 in pieces.
- Idols: exactly seven, maximum 4 in high, on a removable two-level non-combustible stone riser.
- Storage: two lower drawers with removable washable liners.
- Diya tray: fully extendable brass-lined stone tray below the idol riser, with positive open/closed stops and removable drip liner.

### Real-flame and ventilation requirements

- Use quartz, sintered stone or equivalent non-combustible material on the altar, back panel and overhead hot zone.
- Maintain at least 18 in clear above the diya flame and keep timber/photo frames outside the direct heat and smoke plume.
- Place the 2700K LED and its wiring outside the hot zone.
- Provide permanent perforated high and low vents that remain open in both door positions.
- Keep the tray fully extended and the sliding doors fully open whenever diya, incense or dhoop is burning.
- Do not close the unit until every flame and ember is completely extinguished.

## Before fabrication

- Site-measure the finished wall, skirting, floor level, adjacent door swing and stair-side projection.
- Produce coordinated electrical and joinery shop drawings showing socket heights, conduit route, LED driver access, TV ventilation and puja-door pocket thickness.
- Confirm the actual widths and depths of all eight frames and seven idols before fixing the puja shelf and riser dimensions.

## Engineering and daily-use details

### Sofa and charging plank

- Fabricate the long sofa as transportable structural modules joined from below, then finish the seat as one continuous upholstered plane on site. No visible joint, piping break or cushion division is permitted.
- Build the service plank from 18 mm BWP plywood with walnut veneer/laminate over a 50 mm service cavity. Use removable concealed-clip access panels rather than permanently sealing the wiring.
- Set the plank top approximately 650 mm above finished floor and the sofa back approximately 760 mm high.
- Locate each charging faceplate near an exposed end rather than behind a fixed back cushion. Suggested centre height is 590–610 mm above finished floor.
- Use a licensed electrician for the concealed line, earthing and 30 mA residual-current protection. Keep the LED driver accessible at an end panel.

### TV wall

- Mount the 75 in TV bracket to the masonry/structural backing, never only to the stone-look finish or decorative plywood.
- Suggested screen centre is 1100–1150 mm above finished floor for the approximately 9.5–10.5 ft viewing distance.
- Use an approximately 2590 mm-wide × 360–380 mm-deep floating console with a continuous wall cleat or concealed steel frame.
- Provide at least two 32 mm conduits between the TV and the equipment bay: one for power and one for HDMI/data, plus a pull cord and service loop.
- Put the concealed master switch and socket bank inside an accessible end bay. Use perforated intake and exhaust slots and do not place the router or console in an airtight drawer.
- Use anti-glare acrylic rather than glass for the two artworks above the sofa. Keep their lower edge approximately 125–150 mm above the sofa back.

### Pocket-fold puja doors

- With only 13.5 in cabinet depth, ordinary 18–20 in retractable leaves cannot park inside the cabinet. Four narrow leaves are therefore required; each folded pair must be thinner and shallower than the available pocket.
- Use manufacturer-rated synchronized pocket-fold hardware sized for the final brass-frame and 5–6 mm toughened fluted-glass weight. Do not improvise the runner from ordinary wardrobe channels.
- Allow adjustable top and bottom guides, a soft-close centre catch and a removable pocket-side service panel for future hinge/runner adjustment.
- Keep a minimum 3 mm operating gap between leaves and 5 mm running clearance inside each pocket, subject to the selected hardware specification.
- The diya tray should use full-extension, positive-stop runners rated at least 25 kg, with a removable brass drip liner over quartz/sintered stone.

### Puja use sequence

1. Pull each folded door pair forward from its side pocket, unfold and latch at the centre when closing the shrine.
2. To use the shrine, unlatch, fold each pair and push it fully into its side pocket.
3. Pull the diya tray fully forward before lighting the diya, incense or dhoop.
4. Extinguish every flame and ember and allow the hot zone to cool before retracting the tray or closing the doors.

## Movement review

- Hall width is 150 in. A 36 in sofa and 15 in TV console leave approximately 99 in between their fronts.
- With the 20 in-deep coffee table centred, the two long-side walking bands are approximately 39.5 in each, above the 36 in target.
- The sofa return remains at the architect-planned end and does not cross the bedroom doorway.
- The pooja door leaves park inside the 13.5 in cabinet depth. Only the diya tray projects temporarily, during worship, into the open kitchen-side area; the adjacent bedroom door continues to open inward as drawn.
- No dining table, hall sink, open TV equipment or outward-swinging temple door is introduced into the circulation.
